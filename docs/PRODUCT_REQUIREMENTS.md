# Product Requirements

## Product Summary

Royal Force Security Service (Pvt) Ltd provides professional security personnel for residential properties, offices, businesses, events, construction sites, warehouses, institutions, and other approved locations.

The website must help visitors:

- Understand what the company provides.
- Decide whether the company can meet their security needs.
- Trust the company without exaggerated marketing claims.
- Contact the company or request a quotation quickly.
- Apply for legitimate employment opportunities when recruitment is available.

## Primary Audiences

- Homeowners and residential property managers
- Business owners and office managers
- Event organizers
- Construction, warehouse, retail, hotel, and institutional decision-makers
- People seeking security-related employment

## Product Principles

- Trust before decoration
- Clear information before promotional language
- Simple contact paths
- Mobile-first usability
- Verified content only
- Accessible and respectful communication
- Professional appearance without aggressive military styling

## Core Behaviour

- Navigation must be clear, consistent, and keyboard accessible.
- Important contact actions must remain easy to find.
- Forms must provide labels, validation, loading, success, and error states.
- Visitors must never reach a dead end.
- Loading, empty, success, error, and unavailable states must be designed where relevant.
- External contact actions such as telephone, email, maps, and WhatsApp must use verified details.

## Functional Boundaries

- Do not add user accounts, payments, dashboards, live tracking, booking systems, chatbots, or complex administration unless explicitly requested.
- Do not create services, service areas, prices, licences, guarantees, response times, vacancies, or customer relationships that have not been verified.
- Do not collect more personal information than the business needs.
- Do not request identity documents through an ordinary public form.
- Do not add automatic carousels, immediate promotional popups, or newsletter interruptions.

## Experience Requirements

- Use simple, professional English.
- Use familiar controls and understandable labels.
- Provide feedback after meaningful actions.
- Keep important information visible instead of hiding it for visual simplicity.
- Make touch targets at least 44px high or wide where practical.
- Ensure the interface remains usable with long content and missing optional data.

## Acceptance Standard

The completed product must feel like a credible Sri Lankan security-service company website, not a generic template, cybersecurity company, police website, military website, or stock-market product.
