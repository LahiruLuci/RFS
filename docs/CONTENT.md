# Content Source Of Truth

## Status Rules

Replace bracketed values only after the business confirms them. Until then, display an appropriate placeholder in development or omit the item from production.

```text
Legal company name: Royal Force Security Service (Pvt) Ltd
Primary domain: [VERIFY .LK OR .COM]
General email: [VERIFY]
Telephone: 0777356847

Office address: No. 554/C, Panagoda Junction, Homagama.
Business hours: [VERIFY]
Primary operating areas: [VERIFY]
Company registration number: [VERIFY BEFORE PUBLICATION]
Security licences or approvals: [VERIFY BEFORE PUBLICATION]
Social media links: [VERIFY]
```

## Writing Style

- Use clear, professional, simple English.
- Sound confident, calm, helpful, and responsible.
- Prefer specific explanations over vague promotional language.
- Use short headings and readable paragraphs.
- Write for customers first and search engines second.
- Do not use aggressive, fear-based, military, or exaggerated language.

## Prohibited Claims

Do not publish claims such as:

- Number one or best security company in Sri Lanka
- 100% guaranteed protection
- Government approved
- Certified or specially trained officers
- Specific years of experience
- Specific customer, employee, or deployment numbers
- Guaranteed response times

These may be used only when the company supplies reliable evidence and explicitly approves publication.

## Never Invent

- Customer reviews or names
- Partnerships, awards, or client logos
- Licences, registrations, or certifications
- Staff names and biographies
- Office locations or service areas
- Prices and promotions
- Statistics or success rates
- Job vacancies
- Emergency response times

## Content Management

- Keep reusable business details in typed configuration files.
- Do not repeat contact information manually across components.
- Keep reusable marketing and service content in structured content files where practical.
- Record the source or approval status of claims that require evidence.
- Ensure metadata and structured data use the same approved information as visible content.
